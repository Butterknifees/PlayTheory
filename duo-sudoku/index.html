How to play sudoku with two players

The board is an ordinary sudoku: every row, column and 3×3 box must end up holding 1 to 9 once each. The difference is that you share it with someone else, and every number you place is a point for you and not for them.

  1. Place a correct number and you score it. Numbers that still have the most copies left to place are worth the most, so the values shift as the board fills. A strip above the board shows what each number is worth right now.
  2. Claim the bonus squares. Each puzzle has four ×3 squares and eight ×2 squares. A number placed there scores triple or double.
  3. Don’t guess. A wrong number costs you 1 point and the square stays empty for anyone to take. In rapid fire you also score nothing for the next 15 seconds.
  4. Win on points. When the board is full or the clock runs out, the higher score wins. A tie is possible.

Prefer something simpler? Switch to number value scoring, where a correct number scores its own value: a 7 is worth 7 points.

Two ways to play

Rapid fire

The default. No turns: both of you place numbers as fast as you can until the board is full or the game clock ends. Pick 3, 5 or 10 minutes.

Take turns

One number each, in turn, with 30 seconds per move. Add a 3 or 5 minute clock per player if you want the pressure of a chess clock.

Choose Medium for a quick, friendly race or Hard when you both know your sudoku.

Play multiplayer sudoku online

Your friend doesn’t need to be in the same room. Tap Play online, then Challenge a friend and send the link, or Make a room and tell them the 5-letter code. They open it on their own phone or computer, and every number either of you places shows up on both screens straight away.

Sitting together? Put one laptop or tablet between you and play on one screen. Blue and black numbers show who placed what.

Questions

How do you play sudoku with two players?

You both fill in the same puzzle. Each correct number scores points, bonus squares score double or triple, and a wrong number costs a point (in rapid fire, you then score nothing for 15 seconds). When the board is full or the clock runs out, the higher score wins.

Can I play sudoku online against a friend?

Yes. Tap Play online → Challenge a friend and send them the link, or make a room and tell them the 5-letter code. You play the same puzzle live on two devices.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It’s free and there’s no sign-up. You type a name when you arrive and start playing.

How long does a game take?

Rapid fire games last 3, 5 or 10 minutes. 10 minutes is the default.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.
