What makes a good chess alternative?

  • No luck. No dice or shuffled cards, so the better player wins.
  • Nothing hidden. Both players see everything, so you can always plan ahead.
  • Simple rules, deep play. Most of these games take a minute to learn, compared with chess’s six piece types, castling and en passant.
  • Short games. A round often takes five or ten minutes, which makes them great for a break at work or school.

Questions

What is a good alternative to chess?

Look for two-player games with no dice, no cards and nothing hidden, so the better player wins. Amazons, Hex, Clobber, Kōnane, Domineering and Dots & Boxes all work this way, and most have far simpler rules than chess.

What strategy game is easier to learn than chess?

Hex, Clobber and Domineering can each be explained in one or two sentences, yet they stay deep enough for years of play. Hex also can never end in a draw.

What is a quick brain game to play with a friend?

Duo Sudoku: you both fill in the same sudoku and race for points, in games of 3, 5 or 10 minutes. It’s free here, on one screen or online with a link.
